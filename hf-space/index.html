ReleaseSeal

An offline, deterministic verifier of model-release documentation against its artifacts. It emits a hash-chained evidence pack you can re-verify offline.

Evidence, not certification. ReleaseSeal reports consistency and completeness of documentation. It does not certify compliance or "passing". A finding is a place to check, not a verdict.
